Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about visiting us, our menu options, and hosting events.

Walk-ins are always warmly welcomed anytime! If you are planning to visit with a group of 4 or more, or want to guarantee your favorite window table during busy weekend brunch hours, reserving in advance through our form or phone is recommended.

Yes, absolutely! We offer artisan barista oat milk and almond milk for all our coffee and espresso beverages. Most of our bakery items, sandwiches, toasts, and signature bowls are 100% vegetarian.

Yes! We provide dedicated high-speed fiber internet and power outlets along our communal tables and booth seats. Many guests enjoy working, writing, or reading while savoring their favorite brew.
